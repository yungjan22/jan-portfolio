## About this product

I worked with GitHub to research and design for people who aren’t engineers but use vibe coding tools.

## My approach to messy problems

I led the team through different research methods to find answers:

- Online observation
- Survey
- User interviews
- Competitive analysis
- Usability testing of 6 AI tools
- 3 rounds of concept testing

## What we discovered

Participants felt a lack of control over the AI.

> “I’m just asking a question or changing one thing. Why did it change everything?”

## How I solved it

I designed features that gave users more control:

- **Explain and Iterate modes.** Ask about your app without changing it, or change it on purpose.
- **Select a component** to inspect it, edit it or ask about it.
- **A technical level setting**, so explanations match the person reading them.

## The part I’m proud of

We presented our research and three concepts to GitHub. Its July 2025 public preview of Spark launched with an Iterate tab and point-and-select editing, close to two of our three concepts.

## What I’d do differently

GitHub retired Spark in August 2026. Getting to a real, launched app was one of its weak spots, and it was the pain our survey ranked highest, the one I chose to set aside. Next time I’d keep one concept on the hardest pain point, and bring in an engineer to make it testable.
